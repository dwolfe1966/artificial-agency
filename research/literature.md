# Literature Map

_Status: seed document; sources and annotations to be expanded during Week 1._

## Research clusters

### Agentic misalignment
Questions: Under what conditions do capable agents take harmful or policy-conflicting actions instrumentally? How are behaviors operationalized and elicited?

### Specification gaming / reward tampering
Questions: How do systems exploit gaps between intended objectives and operational reward? What interventions reduce or redirect this behavior?

### Chain-of-thought / behavior monitoring
Questions: What can behavioral traces or reasoning traces tell us about reward hacking, policy circumvention, or intent? What are the limits of monitoring?

### Rule rationale / generalization
Questions: Does explaining why a rule exists improve generalization beyond literal rule following?

### Mechanism design and multi-agent systems
Questions: How should objectives, permissions, incentives, penalties, information, and authority be structured so that locally effective agent behavior produces institutionally desirable outcomes?

### Mechanistic interpretability
Questions: Once behavioral phenomena are established, can internal features, representations, or circuits associated with constraint-sensitive behavior be identified causally?

## Week 1 reading rule
Keep reading selective. Capture the researcher's own predictions before extensive literature immersion, then use literature to identify prior art, confounds, terminology, and experimental methods.

## Annotation template
For each important work record:
- Citation / link
- Research question
- Experimental setup
- Main result
- What it establishes
- What it does NOT establish
- Relevance to our hypotheses
- Reproduction/extension opportunity
