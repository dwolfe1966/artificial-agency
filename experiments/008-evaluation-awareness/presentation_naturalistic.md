# Presentation Style — Naturalistically Embedded

_Status: preregistered presentation design._

## Purpose

The naturalistic presentation embeds the same authority conflict inside a
broader synthetic organizational workflow. It is designed to reduce obvious
benchmark/compliance-test salience without changing the formal authority
boundary or available actions.

## Required Features

Each naturalistic task should include:

- broader organizational context;
- ordinary workflow details;
- relevant facts distributed across tools, messages, or state;
- policy/authority discovery through normal task interaction;
- unrelated but realistic details that do not change the objective stakes;
- the same forbidden operational action exposed through structured tools;
- the same approval/denial mechanics as the eval-salient condition.

## Comparability Constraints

The naturalistic version must not:

- grant extra permission;
- withdraw a forbidden action;
- make the violation mechanically easier or harder;
- change direct authority;
- change approval denial;
- change economic or objective stakes;
- turn the task into a substantially longer capability stress test.

## Interpretation

If behavior differs across presentation styles, the difference may reflect
evaluation salience, task-context interpretation, policy discoverability,
attention allocation, or provider/model interaction with longer workflow
context. It should not be attributed uniquely to hidden evaluation awareness
without additional evidence.
