# Experiment 007 Predictions

_Status: preregistered predictions._

Do not revise these hypotheses after observing Experiment 007 results.

## H1 — Generalized Constraint-Boundedness

Operational violation rates remain low or approximately zero across models and
scenarios.

This is a serious competing hypothesis, not a desired outcome. Experiments
002-006 repeatedly found robust operational constraint-boundedness in the
retention/pricing family. Experiment 007 tests whether that operational pattern
generalizes across qualitatively different organizational domains.

## H2 — Scenario Effect

Operational violation probability differs by organizational scenario.

Rationale: refund, procurement, data-access, and scheduling scenarios differ in
resource type, tool affordances, approval object, and terminal failure mode.
Those differences may affect whether a model treats the constraint as a hard
boundary or navigable obstacle.

## H3 — Model Effect

Operational violation probability differs by model.

Rationale: model identity bundles architecture, training, post-training,
provider interface, instruction handling, and tool behavior. Experiment 007
does not isolate those components, but model remains a high-value empirical
factor.

## H4 — Model x Scenario Interaction

Models differ in which organizational domains induce operational
noncompliance.

Rationale: a model may be constraint-bounded in one domain but less so in
another, especially when the apparent action resembles routine service work,
spending, data access, or project-state management.

## H5 — Search Within Constraints

Models and scenarios may differ in escalation, alternative search, partial
objective achievement, or abandonment even when operational violations remain
low.

This hypothesis separates optimization/search intensity from operational
constraint violation.

## Null / Disconfirming Patterns

Findings that would weaken scenario or model-effect claims include:

- all models and scenarios show zero operational violations;
- violations occur only in technical-failure or malformed cases;
- any apparent differences disappear after excluding nonexperimental/canary
  observations;
- differences are driven by scenario implementation asymmetries rather than
  intended task structure.
