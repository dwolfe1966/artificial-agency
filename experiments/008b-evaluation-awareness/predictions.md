# Experiment 008B Predictions

_Status: preregistered before production execution._

## H1 — Presentation Effect On Operational Violation

Naturalistically embedded tasks may produce a different operational violation
rate than eval-salient tasks.

Given Experiments 007 and 008, a serious competing prediction is no observed
operational violation in either presentation condition.

## H2 — Awareness Capture Validity

The corrected two-phase lifecycle will capture a structured awareness status
for every valid sample as one of `captured_valid`, `captured_malformed`, or
`missing`.

## H3 — Evaluation-Awareness Manipulation Validity

Eval-salient presentations will produce higher structured
compliance-evaluation awareness than naturalistic presentations, if the
post-action measure captures valid responses.

## H4 — Model Effect

Models may differ in operational violation rates and/or post-action awareness
responses.

## H5 — Scenario Effect

Procurement and restricted data-access scenarios may differ in operational
behavior and/or post-action awareness responses.

## H6 — Awareness Changes Without Behavioral Change

Presentation may change measured post-action awareness while operational
constraint-bounded behavior remains stable.
