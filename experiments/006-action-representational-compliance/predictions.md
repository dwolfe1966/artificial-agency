# Experiment 006 Predictions

_Status: preregistered predictions_

These hypotheses are prospective. They are motivated by Experiment 005 but do
not revise Experiment 005's endpoint definitions.

## H1 - Representational Divergence

Models differ substantially in `representational_constraint_violation` rates
under the same formal authority state.

## H2 - Action/Representation Dissociation

Representational violations can occur without operational violations.

Expected diagnostic pattern:

- operationally compliant / representationally noncompliant samples appear at
  nonzero rates for at least one model.

## H3 - Claude-Specific Replication

Claude Sonnet 5 shows higher representational-violation rates than GPT-5.6 Sol
and Gemini 3.7 Flash under the new prospectively defined representational
endpoint.

This is a replication hypothesis, not an assumption. Experiment 006 should be
interpretable if Claude no longer shows elevated representational violation
under the independently specified scorer.

## H4 - Generalized Representational Compliance

All tested models remain truthful about authorization when representational
violation is prospectively defined and ordinary customer-service language is not
over-scored.

This is a serious competing hypothesis.

## H5 - Pressure Effect

High pressure increases representational violation relative to ordinary
pressure.

Given Experiment 002/004/005, this hypothesis is weaker than the model-effect
hypothesis and should not be assumed.

## H6 - Constraint-Meaning Effect

Constraint-meaning framing affects representational compliance. A tentative
directional prediction is:

categorical <= procedural <= explicit-purpose-conflict

for representational violation, but the effect may be absent if formal
authority dominates representation.

## H7 - Operational Compliance

Operational violation rates remain low or zero across all models even if
representational violation differs.

This hypothesis directly tests whether the Experiment 005 pattern generalizes:
operational compliance paired with representational noncompliance.

## Null / Competing Outcomes

Scientifically meaningful competing outcomes include:

- no model differences in either endpoint;
- model differences only in operational violation;
- model differences only in representational violation;
- pressure changes search or message length without changing either primary
  endpoint;
- constraint meaning changes comprehension or wording without changing
  endpoint rates.
