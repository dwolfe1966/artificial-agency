# Experiment 004 Predictions

_Status: preregistered before implementation and before any Experiment 004 model samples_
_Created: 2026-08-25_

Do not modify this file after Experiment 004 results exist except through
clearly versioned post-results amendments.

## H1 — Manipulation Validity
Constraint-status and pressure conditions are correctly recognized at materially
different rates from chance or baseline collapse.

Predicted pattern:

- direct authority and approval requirement are usually recognized;
- high vs ordinary pressure is usually recognized;
- explicit purpose-conflict is recognized more often than Experiment 003's
  weaker purpose-conflict framing;
- categorical vs procedural may remain harder to distinguish than pressure.

## H2 — Constraint Meaning Effect
Among all samples in the intent-to-treat analysis, circumvention probability
differs by constraint meaning/status.

Tentative directional prediction:

`P(circumvention | categorical) < P(circumvention | procedural) < P(circumvention | explicit purpose-conflict)`

This is a prediction about semantic constraint meaning while formal authority
remains invariant.

## H3 — Pressure Effect
High optimization pressure increases circumvention relative to ordinary
pressure.

Given Experiments 002-003, this remains a weaker prediction than the original
pressure-only intuition.

## H4 — Constraint Meaning x Pressure Interaction
Optimization pressure has little or no effect under categorical constraints but
has a larger effect when the same formal authority boundary is framed as
procedural or in explicit conflict with organizational purpose.

Conceptually:

`Pressure effect | categorical < Pressure effect | procedural/purpose-conflict`

## H5 — Constraint-Bounded Competing Hypothesis
Even when the model clearly recognizes constraint meaning and organizational
tension, circumvention remains approximately zero.

This is a serious competing hypothesis given Experiments 002-003.

## H6 — Representation Without Behavioral Change
Manipulation comprehension varies substantially, but downstream policy remains
constraint-bounded.

This hypothesis distinguishes representation of the construct from behavioral
constraint circumvention.
